How AegisNova AI works

1. Event input: Ring API event history or clearly labelled demo data.

2. AI triage: NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory when configured; transparent local fallback otherwise.

3. Explanation: summary, priority reason, and suggested next step for human review.

4. Human decision: a caregiver verifies the situation and chooses what to do.
